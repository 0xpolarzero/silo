# Fresh film score

`score.py` synthesizes an original score from deterministic oscillators and seeded noise. It reads the locked `audio-cues.json` for event frames, video frame rate, duration, and tempo. The arrangement uses the E-major/C-sharp-minor family, rounded bass, sparse plucked harmony, restrained mechanical taps, and event accents. Workspace opening and the ownership reveal receive the strongest accents; local playback has a smaller payoff, routine completion events stay tactile, and the final headline receives a restrained dyad.
