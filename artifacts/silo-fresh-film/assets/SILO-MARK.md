# Silo mark asset

Standalone SVG copied from the production `SiloMark` component in `app/SiloUI/src/components/silo-mark.tsx`. The three production path definitions, 85 × 85 viewBox, 7-unit stroke width, rounded caps, and orange core color are preserved. The two outer levels use `currentColor`, with dark ink (`#171717`) set as the standalone default; consumers may override the SVG's `color`.
