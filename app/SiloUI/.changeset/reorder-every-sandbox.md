Sandboxes on other computers can now be dragged into place in the sandbox list too. Each computer keeps its own order of the list, for its own sandboxes and remote ones, and reordering no longer changes the sandbox configuration. Sandboxes that need attention still stay at the top. A sandbox's ⋯ menu also has **SSH**, after **Storage**, to open its SSH tab.
