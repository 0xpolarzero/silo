A few things to know.

Do I need to change my editor?+

No. Choose your preferred editor and terminal in Silo. Open a project from Files, or use SSH access with a compatible editor or coding agent.

How do remote computers work?+

Install Silo on both computers. Enable SSH and Allow remote management on the host, then connect from your computer. Keep Silo running on the host. Manage its GitHub account, secrets, and backups there.

Remote setup guide →
What happens when I stop a sandbox?+

Your files survive stops, restarts, and app updates. Quitting Silo stops its local VMs and preserves their files; VMs on other computers keep running.

Do I need to connect GitHub?+

No. GitHub is optional. You can create a sandbox and work in its terminal without connecting an account.
