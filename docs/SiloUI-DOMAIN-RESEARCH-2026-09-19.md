## Recommendation

Choose silo.codes for a clear developer-product identity, or silo.zip for brevity and a packaging/isolation association. The latter also resembles a filename. silo.place is the least expensive verified neutral option by renewal price. These are editorial judgments.

## TLD landscape

There is no finite official category of “TLDs used by startups.” The useful distinction is repurposed country codes versus newer generic domains; country codes were historically used, just not primarily as global software branding.

Country-code origins (broad naming shortlist, not equal adoption):
.ai Anguilla; .io British Indian Ocean Territory; .co Colombia; .so Somalia; .sh Saint Helena; .gg Guernsey; .to Tonga; .me Montenegro; .fm Federated States of Micronesia; .tv Tuvalu; .vc Saint Vincent and the Grenadines; .ly Libya; .cc Cocos (Keeling) Islands; .is Iceland; .it Italy; .im Isle of Man; .am Armenia; .ac Ascension Island; .in India; .id Indonesia; .rs Serbia; .la Laos; .cx Christmas Island; .ws Samoa; .nu Niue; .re Reunion; .pm Saint Pierre and Miquelon; .tf French Southern Territories; .do Dominican Republic; .je Jersey.
